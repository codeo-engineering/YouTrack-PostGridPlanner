Description

Post Grid Planner turns a YouTrack project into an Instagram content planner. A monthly calendar sits next to an Instagram-style profile grid, so your team can schedule posts and see how the feed will look before anything goes live. Every post is a regular YouTrack issue, so the plan, the visuals and the status stay where your team already works.

Key features

  • Calendar and grid side by side. Posts are ordered by Publish Date, newest on top, just like a real profile. With more than 9 posts the grid scrolls.
  • Images from the issue. Attach an image to an issue and it appears in the grid. Posts without an image show as an empty tile you can click to add one.
  • Carousels and cover images. Several images make a carousel. Choose the cover with "cover" in the file name or in the comment you attach the image to.
  • Drag to reschedule. Move a post to another day and the grid reorders itself.
  • Several Instagram accounts. Switch between one feed per value of a Product, Department or Brand field.
  • Boosted posts. Posts with a running ad (between Ad Start Date and Ad End Date) get a blue Boosted label.
  • One-click setup. A Project Settings tab adds the fields the planner needs after you confirm.
  • Native look. Follows your YouTrack theme and uses YouTrack's own colors and controls.

Privacy

The app only calls the YouTrack REST API as the signed-in user. No data is sent outside YouTrack.
